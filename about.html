Our story

Interventional decisions are made on images. We think clinicians should be able to see what the vessel wall is made of, not just its outline.

Luminvue grew out of intravascular OCT research at CUHK's Department of Biomedical Engineering, working closely with interventional cardiologists at the Department of Medicine & Therapeutics. Joint studies since 2022 showed what higher resolution could add in the cath lab, and where current catheters fall short.

We founded the company in 2024 to turn that work into a product: a 1060-nm imaging platform with liquid-shaped micro-optics, depth-uniform image reconstruction and a torque-coil-free drive, with a catheter slim enough for intracranial arteries.

Today the portable console and catheter prototypes have imaged porcine coronary arteries ex vivo and in vivo. We are now carrying out bench characterisation and quality-system preparation on the way to clinical studies.

01

Resolution that changes decisions

Finer axial and lateral detail to show thin fibrous caps, micro-calcification and thrombus.

02

Built for small, tortuous vessels

A catheter of 0.5 mm or less and a coil-free drive designed for the intracranial circulation.

03

Designed for adoption

Simpler optics and assembly to bring down disposable cost for high-volume and regional centres.

Roadmap

From prototype to first-in-human.

Development is planned verification-first, with quality-system groundwork and biocompatibility testing ahead of clinical studies.

  1. 2024

    Company founded

    Luminvue Medical Limited incorporated in Hong Kong as a CUHK spin-off.

  2. 2025

    Prototype & animal imaging

    Portable 1060-nm console and liquid-shaped catheters built. Ex vivo and in vivo porcine imaging completed.

  3. 2026

    Characterisation & quality

    Bench characterisation, ISO 13485 / IEC 60601 gap assessment and ISO 10993 biocompatibility testing.

  4. 2027

    Partners & design v2

    International exhibition debut, clinical site engagement and next-generation console and catheter.

  5. 2028+

    Clinical studies

    Early feasibility studies at partner centres, followed by registration in China and other markets.
